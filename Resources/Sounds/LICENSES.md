Processing (all files): downmix to mono, cut from the original 48 kHz / 24-bit FLAC, onset
trimmed to ~1 ms, short fade-in (0.5 ms) and cosine fade-out, level normalization, resampled
to 44.1 kHz / 16-bit with triangular dither.
