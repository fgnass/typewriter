Privacy

Typewriter is a free, non-commercial hobby project. There are no ads, nothing is for sale, and there is no business behind it.

Who is responsible

Felix Gnass · E-Mail

The app

Typewriter runs only on your Mac. It reads your keys only while the page is open. When you close the page, the text goes into the field you came from, or to the clipboard.

  • No text, key press or other content is sent to me or to any server.
  • Typewriter saves no files with your text. Only the last page stays in memory, for Copy Last Page in the menu, until you quit the app.
  • To insert the text, Typewriter keeps a copy of your current clipboard in memory, puts the text on the clipboard for a moment, presses ⌘V in the app you came from and then puts your old clipboard back. For this it needs the Accessibility permission. It also uses the permission to see if the focus is in a text field. Apart from your clipboard, it reads no content from other apps.
  • What happens to the text after that is outside of Typewriter: the app you write into keeps it, and a clipboard manager or Universal Clipboard (Handoff) can keep or sync it while it is on the clipboard. Text that goes to the clipboard (when there is no text field) stays there until you copy something else.
  • The settings (color, sound, light, Do Not Disturb) are kept in the normal preferences of the app on your Mac. They contain none of your text.
  • The app has no analytics, no crash reports and no update checks. It does not connect to the internet.

Do Not Disturb

This setting is off by default. When you turn it on, Typewriter makes a shortcut named “Typewriter Focus” and asks the Shortcuts app to add it. The shortcut only reads the current Focus and turns Do Not Disturb on or off. Typewriter runs it with the shortcuts command when the page opens and closes. Your text is never given to the shortcut. Shortcuts is part of macOS; what Apple does with your shortcuts, for example with iCloud sync, is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.
